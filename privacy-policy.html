Privacy Policy

What we collect

To process an order we collect your name, phone number, delivery address, and, where you provide one, your email. If you create an account, we store your profile name and email with Firebase Authentication.

How we use it

We use this information only to fulfil and communicate about your order, and to maintain your account if you have one. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Where it\u2019s stored

Account and order data is stored securely with Firebase. Product images are hosted through Cloudinary. Access to order and account data is restricted so only you and store administrators can view it.

Anonymous browsing

You can browse and shop without creating an account. We use a lightweight anonymous session so your cart and wishlist work smoothly, without collecting personal information until you choose to check out.
